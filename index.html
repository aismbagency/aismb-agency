03 — The ladder

Diagnose. Deploy. Measure. Operate.

  1. 1–2 weeks

    Sprint

    Map the work. Rank what is worth automating. Choose one platform. Leave with a pilot plan.

  2. 2–4 weeks

    Workspace

    Stand up a governed ChatGPT or Claude system the client owns, with knowledge, roles, and training.

  3. 4–8 weeks

    Pilot

    Connect one workflow. Test it. Compare it to the baseline. Writes are earned, not assumed.

  4. Monthly

    Operate

    Review usage, cost, and failures. Update instructions. Train. Expand only what is working.
